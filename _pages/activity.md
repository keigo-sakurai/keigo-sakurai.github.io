## Data Mining Summer Seminar

I organize the **Data Mining Summer Seminar**, a seminar series that invites leading young AI researchers in Japan to give invited talks and exchange ideas with students and researchers. The seminar has been held twice so far, and I plan to continue it in the coming years.

### 2nd Seminar — July 11, 2026, Hokkaido University

**Speaker:** [Rikiya Takehi](https://rikiyat.github.io/) ([X](https://x.com/rikiyatakehi)), Ph.D. student, MIT Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence Laboratory (CSAIL)

**Talk:** Research on information retrieval and large language models (LLMs)

### 1st Seminar — September 7, 2025, Hokkaido University

**Speaker:** [Takehiro Takayanagi](https://sites.google.com/socsim.org/izumi-lab/members/student/takehiro-takayanagi) ([X](https://x.com/TTsamurai2)), Ph.D. student, Graduate School of Engineering, The University of Tokyo (currently CEO of [Simulacra Inc.](https://simulacra.co.jp/))
